# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers; help them learn.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate.
